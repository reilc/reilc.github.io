This is the structure I use for every writeup. Copy `_writeup_template.md`
from the repo root into `_posts/` and fill it in — see the README for the
exact workflow.

## Challenge Info
- **Name / Platform**
- **Category**
- **Points / Difficulty**
- **Tools used**

## The Challenge
What was given — the prompt, files, source code, binary, whatever you
started with.

## Initial Approach
What you tried first, including dead ends. This is the most valuable part
for future-you — the reasoning, not just the answer.

## The Vulnerability / Concept
What's actually going on under the hood. This is where you explain the
*design intent* — why the challenge is built this way, what concept it's
testing.

## Solution
The actual steps that got the flag. Code, commands, screenshots.

## What I'd Do Differently
Anything you'd approach faster next time, or a related concept worth
studying further.
